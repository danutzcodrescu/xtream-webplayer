# Xtream Webplayer

A personal live-TV player with multiple users and provider Playlists.

## Language

**Playlist**:
A user's configured provider account, containing its Channels and Categories.

**Channel**:
A television stream belonging to a Playlist and identified within that Playlist.

**Category**:
A provider-defined grouping of Channels, with user-controlled ordering and visibility.

**Programme**:
A scheduled broadcast on a Channel with a title, start time, and end time.
_Avoid_: Program, show (when referring to a scheduled broadcast)

**EPG**:
The electronic programme guide: schedule information for a Playlist's Channels.

**TV guide**:
The browsable view of Channels and their Programmes.

**Catchup**:
Replay of a past Programme when the provider makes its recording available.
